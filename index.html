      About this platform

      How the system works

      A gap in women’s data closes when three groups meet: a researcher who will design the study, a fund that will pay for it, and the women it is about. The platform is what puts a named gap in front of all three. It is one stage of that loop, not the whole of it — the loop is drawn below, and each stage says whether it is built or planned.

      1. Gaps are found, one at a time

        Published reviews naming data that is missing about women are found, checked against the live web to see whether it has been collected since, and admitted only after a person confirms the claim against its source.

      2. The platform shows what is missing

        Which data is needed, what status it has, who said it was missing and when, and when the platform last checked.

      3. A researcher designs the study

        The platform hands over a draft design — which women, which variables, which breakdowns — labelled AI-generated and unreviewed, and withheld where the rules refuse one. Reviewing it and running the study is the researcher’s work.

      4. A fund chooses a study to back

        A funder sees the gap, the published claim it rests on and the proposed study in one place, and decides what to pay for. Without this stage the loop has a study nobody funds.

      5. Matching women join the study

        Women whose own gap the study would close take part in it.

      6. The collected data closes the gap

        The record’s status changes, and the loop returns to stage 2.

      How a record enters the system

      Stage 1 in full. The last step is a person: nothing reaches the register until somebody has read the claim against its source and admitted it.

      1. Discover

        Run by plain code

        Europe PMC, searched for the sentences a review writes when it looked and found nothing.

      2. Filter

        Run by AI

        One yes/no question per abstract: does this name missing data about women that the paper does not itself go on to collect?

      3. Extract

        Run by AI

        Structured output against the record schema. What the source does not support comes back null, not a guess.

      4. Verify

        Run by AI

        A live search for data collected since the gap was named. The check is dated, and no search proves a negative.

      5. Design

        Run by AI

        A possible study, generated and kept out of the register — and withheld entirely where the safety rules refuse it.

      6. Validate

        Run by plain code

        Ordinary JavaScript, no model. A claim that does not quote its source word for word is rejected.

      7. Review

        Run by a person

        The run stops. Nothing reaches the register until a person has decided on it, and every decision and its reason is logged.

      Most tools point a model at a problem and let it generate the answer. This one inverts that. Where the data gap is, is a claim, and every claim here is borrowed from a published source and attributed. AI is used for finding, extracting and checking — never for making the claim. Nothing the model composes is stored as a record: a study design is generated when a researcher asks for one, labelled AI-generated, and kept out of the register entirely.
